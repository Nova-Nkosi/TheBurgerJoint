What is
Charlie's?

A little diner with no menu prices

Charlie's isn't a real restaurant. It's ten people who found each other, pulled up chairs, and made a table. Some nights we laugh until it hurts. Some nights we FIGHT and make up. Some nights we just vent. Every night, somehow, we learn something. This is our corner of the internet — text only, warm as a booth by the window.
